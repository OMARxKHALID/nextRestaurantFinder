# Restaurant Finder

## Description
Discover the perfect dining spot with our Restaurant Finder app. Explore a variety of cuisines, from traditional to western favorites, using Google Maps integration.

## Features
- **Search:** Find restaurants based on location, cuisine, or restaurant name.
- **Filter:** Narrow down your search results by price range, ratings, or dietary preferences.
- **Google Maps Integration:** View restaurant locations and get directions directly within the app.
- **Reviews and Ratings:** Read user reviews and ratings to make informed decisions about where to dine.
